## Who am I?

I am a wife, mother of three sons, and a journalist who creates stories from conversations with people.

## What do I do?

Often, the conversation itself can heal a lot.

When we share our stories, they gain meaning and direction through our perspectives and experiences.

## Who am I not?

I am not a psychologist, psychotherapist, or coach.

So you don't have to feel like a patient or client when talking to me.

Let's talk together. It will be about your story, but you won't be alone in it.
