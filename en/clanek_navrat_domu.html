Coming home – Why I left my job at an after-school club

From the series Returning to motherhood and healthy eating

We often only fully appreciate what we have when we step away from it or begin to lose it. I recently went through that experience myself after deciding to take a job at a school's after-school club.

“Although I found understanding and inspiration in how my colleagues presented themselves, my heart was withering in that environment. The support I truly needed to return to my children came from my family: my mum, my mother-in-law and the women close to me.”

Going to work to escape the stress

The beginning of my maternity leave was incredibly demanding. I felt that looking after the children and the home was more than I could manage. So I followed my friends' advice to go back to work, hoping it would ease the mental strain. In one respect, it really did help: I found myself among a wonderful group of women. They inspired me not only because they were mothers too and understood my everyday worries, fears and stress, but also because of how they cared for themselves. They dressed beautifully and looked great.

When your heart is withering

Although I found understanding at work and inspiration in my colleagues' style, emotionally I felt worse and worse. My heart was withering in that environment. I realised that going out to work hadn't given me the answer or the inner relief I needed.

The real support came from my family

While my friends had initially encouraged me to go back to work, I found the support I needed to return home elsewhere. It came from my mum, my husband's mum and, above all, the women in our family. They didn't push me to stay in a job at the cost of my own unhappiness. Instead, they strongly encouraged me to listen to my feelings and go home to my children. With that support, I decided to resign. I am taking this valuable experience with me as I return to caring for my family full-time.
